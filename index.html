Aqui tudo flui · Never ends.

Flui-Flui is a studio of original animated videos made with code: hypnotic 3D loops that never end, and the series GutGuts: The Battle, about a silent swordsman. Every video, soundtrack and sound effect is our own. No third-party music.

The Flui-Flui publishing tool

We use our own publishing tool to post finished videos to our own accounts: YouTube (@fluiflui), Instagram and TikTok. For TikTok, the creator signs in with TikTok, reviews the video and caption, chooses who can view it and which interactions are allowed, and then posts it. Nothing is posted without that confirmation, and the tool uses only the data needed to publish.
