Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Flui-Flui ("we") publishes original animated videos to social media accounts owned by Flui-Flui. This policy explains what data the Flui-Flui tool handles when it connects to TikTok.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, username, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope, to show which account will receive a post.
  • Creator posting settings (privacy options, interaction settings, maximum video length), to show the correct choices before posting.
  • Access and refresh tokens, to publish videos the account owner has approved.
  • Post status returned by TikTok after publishing.

How we use it

Only to upload and publish videos that the account owner has reviewed and confirmed (scopes video.upload and video.publish). We do not read followers, messages, other users' content or analytics, and we do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on Flui-Flui's own computer, in a local file that is not shared or uploaded anywhere. We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. We do not run any server that stores TikTok user data.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the connection is active. You can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services) or by contacting us; we then delete the stored tokens within 7 days.

Children

The tool is not directed to children under 13 and is used only by Flui-Flui.

Contact
